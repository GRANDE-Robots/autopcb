# v4.1 stackup reference

The saved v4.1 PCB uses six copper layers in order F.Cu, In1.Cu, In2.Cu, In3.Cu,
In4.Cu, and B.Cu. The native stack models 0.035 mm outer copper, 0.061 mm inner
copper, and a total thickness of 1.67496 mm including the modeled solder masks.

The recorded fabrication candidate is JLC061612-1080 at 1.6 mm nominal with
1 oz outer / 2 oz inner copper. Obtain factory confirmation of the
exact stack, finished thickness, and copper weights. Evaluate the 90 Ω USB and
100 Ω Ethernet impedance targets and return paths on the accepted build.
The [current stackup record](../STACKUP_CURRENT.json) includes the saved
dielectrics and the archive reference for earlier calculator evidence.

The [fabrication notes](../../../deliverables/fabrication_notes.txt) specify
selective filled and capped vias and document the remaining factory checks.
The controlled release remains on hold.
