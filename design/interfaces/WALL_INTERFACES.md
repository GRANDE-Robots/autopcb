- Regulated power: [Phoenix Contact 1456298](https://www.phoenixcontact.com/en-us/products/device-connector-rear-mounting-sacc-4fe-dsi-m12msl-fsl-m16-gr-1456298) is a 4+FE L-coded M12 feedthrough rated 16 A and 63 V DC; its two sides are male and female, so it does not by itself meet the previous female/female preference. - Signal and IMU combined cable: [Mencom MDC-8FFR-TM](https://www.mencom.com/micro-series-connectors/female_2-through_panel_mount.html) is an A-coded 8-position female/female M16-mounted candidate rated 2 A and IP67; verify its mated seal and mechanical compatibility. 8 or more positions are a preliminary range; all nine CA-MP2 contacts would require at least nine electrical contacts plus suitable shield treatment. [Phoenix Contact 1442188](https://www.phoenixcontact.com/en-pc/products/device-connector-rear-mounting-saccbp-fs-12con-m16-10-pvcsco-1442188) illustrates a shielded 12-position A-coded panel socket, but has a prewired tail rather than the preferred female/female wall interface and is not established as USB-qualified. The stock Xsens cable USB-shield limitation must be resolved independently. Do not assign the same mechanically mating key to electrically incompatible ports.
- USB-C: [ES&S STE-IP67-V2](https://www.esskabel.de/en/product/ste-ip67-v2-usb-c-f-usb-c-f-panel-m12/) is a circular female/female USB-C panel candidate with an M12 mounting thread. The cited page establishes IP67 with its protective cap; waterproofing while a cable is connected is not established and must be checked with the actual mating system.

These products demonstrate similar circular housings, not interchangeability or an approved wall layout. Codes, gender, F/F preference, minimum separation, panel cutouts, shields, cable bends and installed depth must be checked for each selected part. Different pin counts alone are not proof that connectors cannot cross-mate.
